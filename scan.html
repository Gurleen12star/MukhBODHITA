🧬 Why is Sample 1 Flagged as Malignant?

🔬
Elevated VOC signature: MQ-135 detected 3.28 V vs a healthy baseline of ~1.1 V. Acetaldehyde, formaldehyde, and isoprene concentrations are 2.9× above normal thresholds — hallmark biomarkers of OSCC (Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma).
⚡
Abnormally low tissue impedance: Malignant tissue has higher water content, disrupted cell membranes, and altered ionic concentration — causing impedance to drop from ~90 kΩ (healthy) to 14.2 kΩ. This is a well-documented electrophysiological marker of OSCC.
📈
Phase angle deviation: A phase angle of 0.78 vs 1.74 in healthy tissue indicates compromised cell membrane integrity — a biophysical signature consistent with pre-malignant or early-stage carcinoma.
🤝
Multimodal convergence: Both independent sensors — gas VOC array and bio-impedance — independently agree that Sample 1 is anomalous. This dual-modal agreement significantly reduces false-positive probability and increases clinical confidence.
