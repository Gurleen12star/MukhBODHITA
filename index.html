The Science

Two-Step Multimodal Detection

MukhBODHITA performs two simultaneous non-invasive physical tests, fused by edge AI into a single Oral Wellness Score.

01

VOC Gas Sniff Test

Patient exhales through a disposable straw. The MQ-135 sensor detects acetone & ketone volatile organic compounds — chemical biomarkers released by early tumor metabolism.

02

Bio-Impedance Tissue Scan

The copper probe wand touches oral tissue. Healthy cells block micro-current (high resistance). Cancerous ulcers have ruptured membranes that allow current to pass freely (low resistance).

Late-Fusion Edge AI

Both signals are weighted and fused entirely offline on the ESP32 into a single Oral Wellness Score (0–100). No cloud. No internet. Works anywhere in India.

Hardware

Built With Accessible Components

Every component is off-the-shelf, affordable, and field-proven for rural Indian conditions.

⚙️

ESP32 Dev Kit

The AI brain — collects multimodal sensor data, runs offline ML threshold mapping, and streams results via Bluetooth Low Energy.

MQ-135 Gas Sensor

Electronic nose mounted in a 3D-printed PVC chamber. Reads VOC biomarkers from exhaled breath via a ₹1 disposable straw.

Copper Bio-Probe
